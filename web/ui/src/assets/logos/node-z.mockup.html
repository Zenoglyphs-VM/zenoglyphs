Node Z

A single-weight Z route between four small nodes, filled/stroked with the plasma gradient. Chosen over four other concepts (and the original angular Z) for reading cleanly from a 16px favicon tab through the header lockup, and for echoing the design system's network glyphs — a route traced between nodes — closer than a drawn letterform does.
